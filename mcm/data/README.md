# data/

Everything here is local and is not committed (see `.gitignore`).

- `synthetic/`: radio feeds written by `mcm synth`. Safe to share; no real person is in them.
- `captures/`: JSON Lines captures from the simulator or from radios. A capture from real radios
  carries the positions of people in the field. Nothing in this folder leaves this machine, and
  nothing from a real person is captured until the retention rule (R-11) exists.
